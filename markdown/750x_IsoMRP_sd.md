**FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS:**

**1. What's a Meal Replacement?**
As the name suggests, you take a balanced meal replacement instead of eating a regular
meal. They're a quick and convenient way to treat your body to a bounty of high quality
protein, carbohydrates, and a host of other important essential metabolic nutrients.

**2. Why take a meal replacement instead of eating a full regular meal?**
There are four general reasons for taking a meal replacement: (1) you're trying to lose


weight; (2) you're trying to gain some weight; (3) you want a convenient, nutritious,
high-quality meal substitute when you are too busy to make a meal for yourself, or go out
for a meal; or, (4) you are participating in an active body-building and/or exercise

program.

**3. Why are BCAAs beneficial?**
BCAAs (Branched Chain Amino Acids) comprise of 1/3 of muscle tissue and are the first
amino acids to be catabolized due to intense exercise. Therefore, dietary BCAA
supplementation is particularly important for hard-training individuals looking to
maximize their muscle gains.
