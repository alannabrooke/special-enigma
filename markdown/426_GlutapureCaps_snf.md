**GLUTAPURE CAPSULES**

Serving Size: 5 Capsules

60 servings

Amount Per Serving:
L-Glutamine 5g

Directions: Take one serving (5 Capsules) with water or fruit juice two to three times a
day, on an empty stomach. On training days, take one serving immediately after training.

Other Ingredients: Gelatin and magnesium stearate.
