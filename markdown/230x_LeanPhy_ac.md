**Lean Physique**


Clinically tested Svetol helps reduce body weight by regulating glucose levels, as well as by
regulating the absorption of glucose in the intestines. By inhibiting the activity of glucose-6phosphatase, Svetol limits the release of glucose into the general circulation, thus encouraging
the release of triglycerides from adipose tissue. Through this process Svetol induces an increase
in the lean mass to fat mass ration and a reduction in weight. A clinical study of 50 patients
observed an average weight reduction of 5.7% in those who took the clinically researched
amount of Svetol (400mg) found in Ultimate Nutrition Lean Physique. Lean Physique also
contains 12 grams of highly purified protein, 2 grams of fiber, and zero grams of sugar per
serving.
