FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS


1. What are heat shock proteins?


Heat shock proteins are sometimes also known as “stress proteins”. This is because it
was found that they stimulate in response to body physical stress condition; such as high
temperatures. Studies suggest they enhance a cells ability to recover from the stress by
triggering immune responses. Heat shock proteins occur under non-stress conditions
also, and act as observers of the cell proteins, aiding in a cells repair process.

2. What is glycogen?


During digestion carbohydrates are converted into glucose, which is used as an
immediate source of "fuel." Glucose that is not directly used to provide energy is
converted to glycogen. Thus, glycogen is the stored energy, which the body taps into
when energy demands are placed on it.


3. Why Creatine Magnapower [®] ?


Creatine and magnesium both play rolls in part of the process that creates ATP in the
body. Supplementation with creatine has been shown to increase levels of creatine in
muscle, thus making more creatine available for the ATP process. Magnesium acts to
catalyze this reaction. Also, by binding creatine to magnesium, it is less susceptible to
breakdown in the stomach to creatinine, leaving more creatine for uptake into the body.
