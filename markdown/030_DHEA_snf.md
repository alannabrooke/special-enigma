**DHEA-25MG**

Serving Size One Capsule
Servings per container: 100

Amount per capsule: Dehydropiandrosterone 25 mg

Directions: One capsule daily with a meal or as recommended by a health care
professional.

Other Ingredients: dicalcium phosphate, gelatin, cellulose, and magnesium stearate

Warning: Not recommended for pregnant, nursing women or any person who is under 35
years of age. Seek the advice of your licensed health care practitioner before using this
product. Do not use this product if you have prostate problems or any cancerous
condition.
