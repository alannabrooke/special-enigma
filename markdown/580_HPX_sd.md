In addition to TORABOLIC, the following 10 key ingredients in Horse Power X provide these essential
functions


  - Beta-alanine (3.2 g): an amino acid which boosts carnosine levels in muscle tissue, thus improving

strength and lactic acid buffering (enabling muscles to perform longer)

  - Creatine monohydrate (3.5 g): the most studied creatine type increases strength and fat-free mass in

conjunction with heavy resistance training by increasing the regeneration rate of adenosine triphosphate
(ATP), the main energy source for cells within the body

  - L-citrulline (3.0 g): an amino acid and nitric oxide booster which increases vascularity, strength, and

aerobic energy production during high-intensity exercise while reducing post-workout muscle soreness

  - Capsimax [™ ] (100 mg): hot pepper (capsaicinoid) extract which increases diet-induced thermogenesis, the

process by which excess calories are converted to heat

  - Razberi-K [® ] (200 mg): raspberry extract which supports diet-induced thermogenesis and energy

production

  - Caffeine anhydrous (250 mg): a crystalline alkaloid which increases alertness and boosts brain function;

250 mg is roughly equivalent to the amount in 15 ounces of coffee

  - Niacin (niacinamide) (50 mg): a vitamin which stimulates muscle-energy production and supports

healthy joints

  - L-tyrosine (500 mg): an amino acid which supports cognitive function that typically diminishes after

physical and mental exhaustion

  - Vitamins B6 (pyridoxine HCl) (50 mg) and B12 (50 mcg): vitamins which metabolize protein and fat

while converting carbohydrates to sugar for energy
