The human body counts on healthy levels of bacteria in the
gut for several functions, in addition to breaking down foods
and absorbing nutrients. Ultimate Nutrition [®] has developed
Balance help optimize all these processes.


Balance has been formulated with nine specific “live” strains
of bacteria, delivering over 40 billion colony-forming units
(CFU’s) per serving.


Bifidobacterium strains, commonly found in fermented foods
like yogurt and cheese, should be supplemented when where
there is a shortage of normal bacteria. Balance includes
two species of bifidobacterium – bifidum and longum, both
of which have been selected to restore optimum levels of
beneficial bacteria in the gut.



Streptococcus thermophilus is another essential bacteria
that has been thoroughly researched for its various health
benefits, like digestive improvements (including the
breakdown of dairy lactose), immunity support, a decreased
risk of developing kidney stones, and enhancment of HDL
(good cholesterol) levels.
