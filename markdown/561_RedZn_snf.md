Directions: As a dietary supplement, take 3 caplets with 8 oz. water twice daily,
once in the morning and again mid-afternoon. On training days, take one serving
30 minutes before your workout. Do not exceed 6 caplets per day. Do not take
within 6 hours of sleep.


Red Zone contains 100mg of caffeine per serving.

Warning: Too much caffeine may cause nervousness, irritability, sleeplessness
and occasionally, rapid heartbeat. Not recommended for use by children
under 18 years of age.
