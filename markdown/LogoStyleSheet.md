#### **Using Ultimate Nutrition’s Brandmark**

**Original Art**
Ultimate Nutrition logo (the Logo) original drawing
files are EPS (vector) format, which remains clear at
any size. It is the user’s responsibility to convert EPS
files to any other required file types at adequate
resolution, so that the Logo remains perfectly clear,
and never looks out-of-focus, jagged or fuzzy.


**Don’t Change It!**
Our Logo is a registered trademark. It must appear
exactly as drawn, including the registration mark,®.
It must not be altered or distorted in any way.


**Minimum Size**

Although there is no maximum size limit, the Logo
must not be reduced to less than 22mm, or 7/8” wide.


**Colors**

The Logo is only used in three colors: white, black and
PMS2735 (or exact match). PMS2735 is dark violet and
is used on a white background only. Black Logo may
be used on any color background lighter than 40%
black density. Only a white Logo may appear on
higher-density (darker than 40%) backgrounds.


**Clear Space / Shared Space**
The Logo must have adequate clear space around it.
The minimum amount of clear space required is equal
to the height of the Logo small caps combined. The
Logo may not share undefined or empty space with
any other symbol, logotype, device or brand, regardless of clear space.


**Background Contrast / Noise**
The Logo must only appear on a solid or near-solid
color background. Any background texture must be
kept to minimum contrast. Placement on images with
contrast patterns or color variations is prohibited.

**Type Specification**
Recommended typefaces to use with the Logo are
those used in this document: Barlow is preferred for
all written communications, and Stratum1 may be
used, as necessary for display callouts and headline
emphasis. Barlow is an extended family with many
weights and widths, and should be used for most
purposes. Both are Google fonts. Alternatively, set in
an evenly weighted sans-serif such as DIN.
