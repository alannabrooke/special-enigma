**VITAMIN C TABLETS**

Vitamin C, also known as ascorbic acid, is a water-soluble vitamin that is vital for normal
functioning of the body. The human body does not produce vitamin C on its own, nor
does it store it. So it is important to include plenty of vitamin C-containing foods in your
[daily diet. Antioxidants, such as vitamin C and vitamin E, are an important part of the](http://www.vitacost.com/science/nutrients/antioxidants.html)
body’s defense against muscle damage from exercise. Vitamin C also assists the immune
system in two of its primary functions to rid the body of foreign invaders. While exercise
increases the body’s natural defense against free radicals, athletes who are doing intense
[training may benefit from the addition of this antioxidant supplement to their diet.](http://www.vitacost.com/science/nutrients/antioxidants.html)
