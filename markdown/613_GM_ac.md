**Glucosamine MSM**

Osteoarthritis is associated with a breakdown of cartilage in joints and commonly occurs
in the hips, knees and spine. Osteoarthritis occurs when there is significant wearing of
the articular cartilage of the joint. As the articular cartilage is worn down, swelling and
pain occur due to inflammation. MSM and glucosamine, as dietary supplements, are
used together to help ease the symptoms of osteoarthritis. Glucosamine is an amino
sugar normally produced in the human body. It is necessary for the construction and
repair of connective tissue and healthy joint cartilage. MSM is useful for relieving
inflammation and pain in joints that are associated with osteoarthritis, rheumatoid
arthritis, muscle pain, fatigue and back pain. MSM relieves inflammation and pain by
helping to equalize cell pressure.
