FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS


1. Do Various Vitamins and Minerals Work Together?

In a majority of cases, vitamins and minerals do work together and may even reinforce
each other's biological activities.

2. Are Fat-Soluble and Water-Soluble Vitamins Effectively Absorbed?

Yes! The absorption rate may vary depending on the vitamin, but there is no biological
barrier in simultaneous absorption of fat- and water-soluble vitamins.
