High Plank Shoulder Taps


- Assume a push-up position with hands
directly below your shoulders, core tight,
back flat, and feet shoulder width apart.


- Do not move your lower body, pick up one
hand off the ground and slowly tap the
opposite shoulder before bringing it back
to the ground.


- Alternate hands as your tap the opposite
shoulder.





Inverted Row


     - Position yourself underneath a bar at hip
height and brace your core to maintain a
straight line from your feet to your neck.


     - Squeeze your shoulder blades together and
drive your elbows back to pull your chest
to the bar.


     - Slowly lower yourself back down and continue for reps. Don’t bounce off the bottom
or allow your body to sag.











Kettlebell Bent Over Row


      - Assume a hip hinge position with a braced
core and flat back, holding a kettlebell in
each hand.


      - Squeeze your shoulder blades together and
drive your elbow back to bring the weights
to your chest. Lower and repeat.


       - Keep your chest parallel with the ground
throughout the exercise.





Swiss Ball Pike


- Start in a plank position with your feet and
lower legs on a Swiss Ball.


- Using your core strength, drive your hips up
until your body is in a V-shape.


- Hold the top position for a moment before
lowering down. Don’t allow your knees or
elbows to bend or back to sag.





Back Squat


- With a barbell across your upper back and a
braced core, slowly lower your hips back and
down keeping your chest up.


      - When your hip joint is lower than your knee
joint, drive the weight up using your legs.


- Don’t allow your knees to cave in but push
them outward tracking over your toes.





Standing Calf Raise


         - Hold an anchor point at hip to chest height
and feet straight, shoulder width apart.


         - Drive down through the balls of your feet to
elevate your heels.


         - Hold the top position for a moment before
lowering back down.



Farmer's Walk


- From standing, hold a heavy dumbbell in
each hand and set your shoulder blades
back and down.


- Walk forward, stabilizing through your core.


- Don’t allow your spine to collapse and keep
your head and chest up.





Glute Bridge


- Lie on your back with your knees bent and
heels close to your hips.


- Drive through your heels and squeeze your
glutes to lift your hips in the air.


- Hold the top position with a straight line
from you knees to your neck for a moment
before lowering to the start.







Lateral Shoulder Raise


- Stand tall with your shoulder blades back
and down and a tight core, holding a dumbbell in each hand at your sides.


- Raise both dumbbells out to the sides until
they reach parallel with the ground, then
slowly lower back down.


- Do not shrug your shoulders up or bend at
the elbows during the movement.
