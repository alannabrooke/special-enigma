# **Pre Gold**

In today’s competitive landscape, Ultimate Nutrition’s team of scientists has pioneered the next
generation of pre-workout: **Pre Gold** . This innovative approach covers far more than just the
energy boost delivered by the average “stim-bomb.”


**Nitric oxide support** and blood flow optimization are essential components of the modern
training regimen. Clinical studies have shown the efficacy of **L-Citrulline**, a precursor to arginine,
on **increasing plasma nitrate levels and nitric oxide availability** to **optimize pump** during
workouts. Research has shown that supplementation with **Potassium Nitrate resulted in a**
**~846% increase in maximum power output** in athletes when compared to placebo. [7] Recent
studies have shown that the combination of these ingredients form a powerful duo to improve
vascular conductance, delivering more oxygen to muscles when they need it most.


Obviously, no pre-workout formula is complete without an energy source. Ultimate Nutrition’s
innovative approach utilizes a unique blend of ergogenic aids, providing sustained energy.
**Synephrine**, the active ingredient in **Citrus Aurantium**, has been shown to support the
**increased energy expenditure** associated with intense exercise, while also **promoting lean**
**muscle development.** [4 ] Explosiveness and intensity are delivered by a time-released blend of
**Caffeine** and **DiCaffeine Malate** . This two-stage energy matrix has been shown to **increase**
**energy expenditure by 21.5%** in the first hour of training. [1] The second phase of energy propels
athletes to power through the final stages of intense training. Research shows a sustained energy
potential with an additional 10% increase in energy for the second hour of exercise. [1 ]


To enhance the intensity of the energy matrix, a clinically-effective dose of **beta alanine** was
included for **Ultimate power output** . [5] To amplify the effects of beta alanine, Dicaffeine Malate
has been clinically proven to increase total weight lifted and elevate peak power. [3 ] To provide an
extra layer of **focus**, the synergistic pairing of caffeine and **taurine** was utilized.


Vitamin B6 and Vitamin C deliver the ancillary support for maximum performance and to ensure
adequate vitamin replenishment. **Pre Gold** has been formulated to equip athletes with the
necessary PUMP, ENERGY, and POWER for an explosive workout. Get ready for the gold and
get **Pre Gold** .
