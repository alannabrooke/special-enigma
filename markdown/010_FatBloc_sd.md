**Fat Bloc**

People are concerned about how they look and are preoccupied with weight and weight loss.
However, despite this concern about weight, more and more of us are becoming overweight.

It is no surprise then, that we are looking for natural alternatives to weight management and
weight control. Among them, chitosan stands out for its ability to bind fat before the body
absorbs it. Chitosan is a naturally occurring water-insoluble dietary fiber derived from the
complex sugar (polysaccharide) found in the shells (exoskeletons) of shrimp, crabs and other
shellfish.

Chitosan has the capacity to bind lipids and fats. Chitosan fibers differ in their structure from
other fibers in that chitosan possesses a net positive charge. This positive charge attracts negative
charges on the fats, lipids and bile acids. Chitosan dissolves in the stomach and then changes to a
gel form, entrapping fat in the intestine. Chitosan fiber is not metabolized; it is cleared from the
body along with the fat in the fecal material.

It has been suggested that the action of chitosan may be enhanced by as small an amount of
Vitamin C. The synergistic effect leads to an increase in the oil-holding capacity of the chitosan
gel with less likeliness of entrapped fat leaking from the chitosan-fat gel.

This dietary fiber is recommended as an addition to a balanced weight management program of a
balanced diet and exercise. Fibers also play an important role in cleansing the gastrointestinal
tract, which stimulates the digestive processes and promotes intestinal health.

**FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS**

**1. Is There Any Allergic Reaction to Chitosan?**
This product should be avoided by people who are allergic to shellfish.

**2. Under What Condition(s) is the Use of Chitosan is Not Recommended?**
Chitosan is for adult consumption only, and should not be used by pregnant or nursing women.

**3. Does Chitosan Interfere with the Action of Vitamins?**
Being a dietary fiber with numerous beneficial effects chitosan should not interfere with the
action of other wholesome remedies. In fact, chitosan works more efficiently even with small
amounts with vitamin C.

**4. How and When Should Chitosan be Taken?**
Chitosan works best if taken roughly 30 minutes before a meal. It should be taken with generous
amounts of fluids.
