**CREAPURE POWDER**

Serving Size: 5g

200 servings

Supplement Facts:
Amount Per Serving: Creatine Monohydrate: 5 grams

Directions: As a dietary supplement, take one rounded teaspoon, approx. 5 grams and
mixed in 5 ounces of water or fruit juice, two to three times a day, evenly spaced, on an
empty stomach.
