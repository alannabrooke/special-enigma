**ULTIMATE SENSATION BAR**

Our protein bars are formulated to be a great source of several different proteins and
other nutrients, all wrapped up in a quick and easy bar. It is easy to take them with you to
work or school or anywhere that you need to get your protein or satisfy your hunger in a
healthy way. And this sensational treat has 5g or less Net Carbs! Each 85-gram Ultimate
Sensation Bar contains 30 grams of great tasting, high quality protein. The Ultimate
Sensation Bar uses a special protein blend consisting of whey protein; in the form of
whey protein isolate and whey protein concentrate, soy protein, and calcium caseinate.
Calcium Caseinate is a high protein, high calcium nutrient derived from milk. Dairy
calcium is shown to be more easily absorbed and utilized than supplemental forms of
calcium. Research has shown dairy calcium helps regulate fat metabolism and promote
the breakdown of fat. Studies show calcium from dairy sources achieves significantly
better weight reduction than calcium supplements such as calcium carbonate. Increasing
dairy calcium speeds up weight and fat loss.
