_**Ultimate Nutrition Natural Gainz**_


_**Ultimate Nutrition’s**_ _**Natural Gainz**_ is a cutting-edge formula that packs 1290 calories from a


combination of key nutrients. Each six-scoop serving is packed with 60g of protein from multiple


sources, 13g of healthy fats, 240g of high-quality carbohydrates, and 6g of dietary fiber.


The 60 grams of protein in _**Natural Gainz**_ consists of whey protein isolate, milk protein isolate,


hydrolyzed whey protein, micellar casein and whey protein concentrates. These proteins and their


varying absorptions rates deliver an array of potent muscle-building amino acids, helping the body


maintain positive nitrogen retention for extended periods of time.


Oat fiber’s inclusion in _**Natural Gainz**_ aids digestion of the various protein sources. Oats are rich in


dietary fiber and, most importantly, a specific type of fiber called beta-glucan, which is known to help


lower levels of bad cholesterol. [1] The formulation also includes antioxidant-rich avocado oil, the main


source of healthy fats in _**Ultimate Nutrition’s Natural Gainz**_ . According to clinical research, avocado oil


has also been shown to help maintain healthy cholesterol levels, by increasing HDL, the “good”


cholesterol. [2,3] Avocado oil is also a good source of lutein, a nutrient that can improve eye health.


_**Ultimate Nutrition’s Natural Gainz**_ can be dosed according to your daily needs. A two-scoop serving


acts as a protein shake, providing 430 calories containing 20g of high-quality protein. Four-scoops serve


as a meal replacement drink, boasting 860 calories and 40g of protein. The six-scoop serving offers 1290


calories (1320 for Chocolate) and 60g of protein, perfect for mass gaining. It also comes in a wide variety


of flavors, including Vanilla, Peanut Butter & Jelly, Strawberry, Chocolate, and Cookies & Cream.
