# **Ultimate Nutrition** **Blast Caps** **Scientific Description**

“Blast Caps” is a scientifically dosed chewing gum that has been formulated to give you
the energy you need as quickly as possible. Each piece of Blast Caps Energy Gum
contains 100mg of caffeine, which is as much as a shot of espresso! Instead of sipping
on your energy drink or your coffee, Blast Caps Energy Gum releases the 100mg of
caffeine to be absorbed virtually instantly. In as little as 5 minutes, you will feel the
effects! You can take it before your workout or throughout the day if you need a boost! [1]

Additionally, the fresh mint flavored Blast Caps Energy Gum is fortified with Niacin,
Vitamin B6, Vitamin B12 to increase alertness, focus and help prevent a “crash.” It is
calorie free and contains no sugars, so there’s no spike and crash like with most energy
drinks.
