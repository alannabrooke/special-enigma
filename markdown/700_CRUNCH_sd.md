The Ultimate Crunch Bar contains a protein blend to utilize all the functional benefits of
different proteins and help support lean muscle mass. Quality whey protein isolate and

Casein is another milk protein that seems to have a timed-release effect as it forms a gel

peptide form for better absorption (due to peptide transport systems in the digestive tract).
Glutamine is a very important amino acid that has many benefits including supporting
lean muscle mass.

Soy protein isolate has been shown to enhance thyroid hormone output, which can
increase metabolic rate to support fat loss. The isoflavones in soy have been shown to
have numerous health benefits including cholesterol and triglyceride lowering effects. It
contains an excellent ratio of glutamine, arginine, and the BCAA's.

Frequently Asked Questions:

1. What type of protein is in the Ultimate Crunch Bar?

Each 65-gram Ultimate Crunch Bar contains 16 grams of great tasting, high quality
protein. The Ultimate Crunch Bar uses a special protein blend consisting of whey
protein; in the form of whey protein isolate and whey protein concentrate, soy protein
isolate, and calcium caseinate.

2. What is so special about the calcium used in the Ultimate Crunch Bar?

Calcium Caseinate is a high protein, high calcium nutrient derived from milk. Dairy
calcium is shown to be more easily absorbed and utilized than supplemental forms of
calcium.

3. How does calcium caseinate aid in fat loss?

Research has shown dairy calcium helps regulate fat metabolism and promote the
breakdown of fat. Studies show calcium from dairy sources achieves significantly better
weight reduction than calcium supplements such as calcium carbonate. Increasing dairy
calcium speeds up weight and fat loss.
