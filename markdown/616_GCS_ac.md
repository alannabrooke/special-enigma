**Glucosamine Chondroitin**

Osteoarthritis is associated with a breakdown of cartilage in joints and commonly occurs
in the hips, knees and spine. Osteoarthritis occurs when there is significant wearing of
the articular cartilage of the joint. As the articular cartilage is worn down, swelling and
pain occur due to inflammation. Chondroitin and glucosamine, as dietary supplements,
are used together to help ease the symptoms of osteoarthritis. Glucosamine is an amino
sugar normally produced in the human body. It is necessary for the construction and
repair of connective tissue and healthy joint cartilage. Chondroitin sulfate also occurs
naturally in the body. Like glucosamine, chondroitin is a building block of proteoglycans:
large protein molecules that give cartilage elasticity. Chondroitin sulfate acts by attracting
fluid into the proteoglycan molecules and stimulates collagen production.
