**F-16**

Introducing F-16, “the fifth generation of creatine supplements”. This new formula
combines our B-Power and V-Power blends with 100% pure creatine monohydrate in a
delicious easy to use drink. B-Power directly influences muscle strength by buffering
lactic acid build-up in muscle, which can help prevent muscle loss commonly resulting
from acidosis. Carnosine also aids in the volumizing effect of creatine meaning fuller
muscles. By effectively buffering lactic acid, carnosine supports optimal creatine
effectiveness. Creatine not only improves physical performance but also reduces mental
fatigue and improves mental performance. Creatine helps make more "fuel" available to
the muscle in the form of ATP. V-Power contains Taurine, a ‘conditionally-essential’
amino acid. Inositol Hexaphosphate (IP6) appears to improve tissue oxygenation by
changing the dynamics of hemoglobin, which delivers oxygen to the working muscles.
