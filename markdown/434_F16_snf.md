Other Ingredients: Dextrose, Natural and Artificial Flavors, Citric Acid, Sucralose, and

Suggested Use:
1. One serving = two scoops.
2. For the first five days (loading stage); take two servings daily, one serving in the
morning and another serving 12 hours later, or right after you work out.
3. For the next ten days (maintenance stage); take one serving immediately after you
work out. On non-workout days, take one serving in the late afternoon.
4. For best taste, mix each scoop of F-16 with 6 oz. of water. Add more water if the
taste is too strong. F-16 may be stacked with Ultimate Nutrition’s ArgiNOne.
