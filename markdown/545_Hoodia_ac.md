**Hoodia Gordonii:**

Hoodia gordonii, has been investigated for use as an appetite suppressant. This
impressive plant contains a special substance, which acts on the brain by sending a signal
to stop the cravings for food. It has been established that the P57 molecule found in pure
Kalahari Hoodia Gordonii works by mimicking the effect that glucose has on nerve cells
in the brain in effect fooling the body into thinking it is full, even when it is not, thus
curbing the appetite. It's very different from dietary stimulants like Ephedra that are now
banned because of questionable side effects. Hoodia has no stimulant effects at all.
Hoodia use leads to a reduced interest in food, a delay in the time after eating before
hunger sets in again, and feeling full more quickly.
