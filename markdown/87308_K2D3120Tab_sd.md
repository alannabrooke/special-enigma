**Ultimate Nutrition’s Vitamin D3/K2** has been formulated with all 206 bones in the human body
in mind. The formulation team at **Ultimate Nutrition** has hand selected a quartet of ingredients
to strengthen bones as well as improve heart health.


The formulation begins with Vitamin D3, which in a recent clinical study has shown promise to
increase calcium absorption and to reduce the risk of bone fractures [1] . The fortification of bones

Vitamin K2 is also in a synergistic pairing with Vitamin D3. When taken together, studies have
shown a **significant increase** in bone mineral density [3], which represents the strength of the
bone, making it vital for bone health. Magnesium chelate which has been shown in recent

While all of the ingredients are crucial to bone health, Vitamin K2 is particularly effective in
reducing arterial calcification, which is hardening of the heart, by **50%** [5] . **Ultimate Nutrition’s**
**Vitamin D3/K2** is an key part of your daily supplementation for overall health and healthy aging.
