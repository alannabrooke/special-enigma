Directions:
Mix one scoop (30 grams) Ultimate Nutrition [®] Protein Isolate
in 12 fl. oz. (355mL) of water or your favorite juice. Mix thoroughly
and drink immediately. We recommend you mix Protein Isolate
in a cup with a spoon and add the water before the powder.
We do not recommend using a shaker cup or blender as excessive
foaming may occur. For a thicker shake use less water and for a
thinner shake use more water. Use entire container within 30
days of opening. Consume Protein Isolate shakes as needed
during the day and immediately after each workout.
