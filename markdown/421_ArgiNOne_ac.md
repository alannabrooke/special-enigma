**ARGINONE**

Boost Nitric Oxide production by stimulating nitric oxide synthase. Arginine enhances
the L-arginine-nitric oxide pathway during exercise. Taken with citrulline, it reduces
exercise-induced increase in plasma lactate and ammonia. Citrulline promotes aerobic
energy production in muscle, and reduces muscle fatigue by stimulating the body’s
removal of metabolic toxins such as lactic acid ammonia during the urea cycle.

_Available in 90 capsules._
