FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS:

When is the best time to drink an energy drink?

If using an energy drink to aid your workout, the best time to drink an energy drink is
when you are just starting or in the mid-part of your workout. By the time you lose some
of your energy, the supplied energy from the drink will already be in the process of
taking its place.

Can I take Energy Smash with other caffeine containing products?

Energy Smash contains 50mg of caffeine per serving and 150mg per can. It is suggested
you limit the use of other caffeine containing products. Too much caffeine may cause
nervousness, irritability, sleeplessness, and, occasionally, rapid heartbeat.
