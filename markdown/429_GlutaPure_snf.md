**GLUTAPURE POWDER**

Serving Size: 5g

80 servings

Supplement Facts:
Amount per serving: L-glutamine 5g

Directions: As a dietary supplement, take one serving (5g, approx one level teaspoon)
mixed in 150ml (~5 oz) of water or fruit juice, two to three times a day, evenly spaced, or
on an empty stomach. On training days, take one serving immediately after training.
