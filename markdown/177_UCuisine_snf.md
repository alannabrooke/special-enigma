Directions: Add two scoops of Ultimate Cuisine (approx. 72 grams) to 12 ounces of
water. Mix thoroughly, chill and serve. Each scoop weighs approx. 36 grams with a
concentrated caloric value of 139 calories per scoop (over 50% of calories from whey
protein). Fruit, honey & yogurt can be added to make a thicker, full bodied nutrition drink
if desired. Just replace one or two meals daily with delicious and nutritious Ultimate
Cuisine and eat moderately the rest of the day.
