**Vitamin D3**


Due to its neural activity via receptors found throughout various brain tissues, supplementation
with Vitamin D can contribute to mood improvement when initial serum levels are suboptimal.
People with poor exposure to sunlight – during winter months, or at extreme latitudes, for
example – are at higher risk of having insufficient levels of Vitamin D.


Maintaining adequate Vitamin D through supplementation helps to achieve healthy serum levels
and lessen depressive symptoms, thereby contributing to overall mood improvement.
