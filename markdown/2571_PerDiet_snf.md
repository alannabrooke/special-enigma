Directions: As a food supplement, take (2) capsules two times daily, 60 minutes before

or within six hours of sleep.


Please note: Do not use if you are pregnant or nursing. This product contains caffeine
and should not be take by those wishing to eliminate caffeine from their diets. Stop
taking product two weeks before surgery.
