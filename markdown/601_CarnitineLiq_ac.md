**LIQUID CARNITINE**

L-Carnitine is a vitamin-like molecule that serves numerous functions in the body. Its
main function is to stimulate the breakdown of fats. L-Carnitine is an alpha-hydroxy acid
that is primarily located in the muscle. L-carnitine plays a critical role in the metabolism
of fat, contributing to the oxidation of fatty acids and transporting long-chain fatty acids
to mitochondria, where they are processed to help provide energy. It is also believed to
help the body protect itself against disorders of the liver, kidneys, muscles, and
cardiovascular system. Muscle needs a regular supply of carnitine to remain toned and
healthy. Also includes Pantothenic Acid (Vitamin B5).
