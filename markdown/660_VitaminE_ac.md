**VITAMIN E**

Vitamin E is a popular and powerful antioxidant. Vitamin E is effective in preventing the
oxidation of polyunsaturated fatty acids. Additionally, Vitamin E is helpful in the
prevention of oxidation in the lungs, where strong oxidizing agents nitrogen dioxide and
ozone, components of air pollution, are particularly harmful to people exercising.
Vitamin E protects white and red blood cells, helping the body's immune system.
