Ultimate  Nutrition®  Protein  Isolate  2  Scientific  Description


During  times  of  physical  stress,  the  body’s  glutamine  reserves  become  depleted,  but  can  be  replenished
through  glutamic  acid  supplementation.  To  address  this  need,  Protein  Isolate  2  contains  only  the
highest-­‐grade  wheat  protein  isolate,  due  to  its  unique  high  glutamic  acid  content.  Ultimate  Nutrition®
Protein  Isolate  2  now  also  includes  pea  protein  isolate,  which  contains  particularly  high  levels  of  arginine
and  lysine,  both  of  which  are  essential  to  growth  and  muscle  development.

Weightlifters  and  bodybuilders  realize  that  glutamine  is  critical  for  muscle  building  because  it  is  a
nitrogen  donor,  meaning  that  it  moves  nitrogen  around  in  the  body  to  where  it  is  needed.  A  positive
nitrogen  balance  is  necessary  to  gain  muscle  mass.  Arginine  also  plays  a  role  in  maintaining  nitrogen
balance,  thereby  supporting  muscle  mass  increase  and  body  fat  reduction.

Glutamine  is  the  ultimate  anti-­‐catabolic.  It  prevents  “muscle  wasting”  by  reducing  the  degree  of  muscle
damage  and  soreness  caused  by  heavy  exercise.  Glutamine  also  helps  the  body  to  secrete  its  own
growth  hormone,  and  subsequently,  the  release  of  IGF-­‐1  (Insulin  Growth  Factor  1).  This  hormone  helps
promote  weight  loss,  increase  lean  body  mass,  and  increase  bone  density.  Glutamine  can  also  neutralize
lactic  acid  burn  in  muscles  by  releasing  plasma  bicarbonate,  allowing  a  longer  workout.


Arginine  stimulates  the  release  of  growth  hormone  from  the  anterior  pituitary  gland,  and  the  release  of
insulin  from  the  pancreas,  which  transports  nutrients  into  muscle  cells  to  promote  growth  while
preventing  the  onset  of  a  detrimental  catabolic  state.


Lysine  stimulates  fat  mobilization  for  energy  uses,  while  supporting  bone  and  muscle  growth  by
maintaining  healthy  calcium  absorption.  Lysine  also  assists  in  recovery  and  the  maintenance  of  lean
body  mass  by  repairing  damaged  tissues  and  supporting  the  body’s  production  of  antibodies,  hormones,
enzymes,  and  collagen.


Enzymes


Ultimate  Nutrition®  Protein  Isolate  2  includes  a  blend  of  three  digestive  enzymes  (protease,  bromelain,
and  papain)  to  aid  in  digestion  and  increase  nutrient  absorption.  These  proteolytic  enzymes  breakdown
the  protein  (macromolecule)  into  smaller  fragments  (polypeptides),  which  are  then  broken  down  into
smaller  peptides  and  individual  amino  acids  which  can  then  be  easily  absorbed  and  utilized  by  the  body.


Due  to  its  broad  specificity  [wide  pH  range]  bromelain  exhibits  activity  through  multiple  areas  in  the
digestive  tract,  including  the  stomach  and  small  intestine.  Additionally,  it  has  been  shown  to  be  an
adequate  replacement  for  the  more  common  animal-­‐source  enzymes  such  as  pepsin  and  trypsin.


Papain  and  protease  are  also  very  effective  in  the  breakdown  of  protein.  They  exhibit  both  endo-­‐  and
exopeptidase  activity,  meaning  they  break  down  the  macromolecules  by  internally  cleaving  them  into
smaller  fragments  [chopping  it  at  the  middle]  and  by  terminally  cleaving  the  ends  of  those  fragments.
