**LEUCINE**

**L-leucine Supplement**

Each 1-scoop serving of Ultimate Nutrition’s **Leucine** features 5 grams of rapid
absorbing, free-form L-leucine. Overall, three amino acids are classified as
branched chain amino acids (BCAAs) based on their molecular structure: leucine,
isoleucine, and valine. During strenuous exercise, leucine is the BCAA that
depletes most rapidly, thus making it the most crucial to replenish. Specifically,
blood leucine levels decline by about 30% during both strength training and cardio
workouts. Supplementing with leucine to increase availability has several benefits
related to muscle protein synthesis (MPS), including activating the main muscle
building pathway, mTOR, for up to 24 hours post-workout. Leucine in its freeform state is advantageous in that it doesn’t take as long to digest as leucine
derived from food. This enables L-leucine to pass quickly through the stomach and
intestine and directly into the bloodstream for faster absorption. Add a scoop to
your favorite protein shake or sports drink to replenish diminished leucine reserves
and enhance muscle growth and recovery!
