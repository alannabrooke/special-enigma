Ultimate Burners is a balanced lipotropic formula to be used as a dietary supplement to
aid fat loss. Ultimate Burners is a combination of Vitamin B6 along with several amino
acids to help build protein and strength, while increasing stamina. Ultimate Nutrition’s
formula is designed to enhance liver function and increase the flow of fats and bile from
the liver and gallbladder. Lipotropics prevent an abnormal accumulation of fat in the
liver. Besides improving fat metabolism, lipotropics also aid in the metabolism of fat
soluble nutrients and aid the liver in its other metabolic functions such as the processing
of hormones, estrogen, sulfa drugs, glucose, and glycogen. Ultimate Burners works by
increasing production of lecithin in the liver, helping to keep cholesterol more soluble
and lessening deposits in the blood vessels. It prevents accumulation of fats in the liver
and it detoxifies amines, which are by products of protein metabolism.


Vitamin B-6 is a water soluble vitamin and is required for the proper absorption of
vitamin B12, and for the production of hydrochloric acid and magnesium. Vitamin B6
helps linoleic acid function better in the body and plays a major role in the breakdown
and utilization of carbohydrates, fats, and proteins. In addition, vitamin B6 facilitates the
release of glycogen from the liver and skeletal muscles so that it can be used for energy.
Vitamin B6 also helps to maintain the balance of sodium and potassium, which regulate
body fluids and promote the normal functioning of the nervous system and skeletal
muscle system.


Choline aids in emulsifying cholesterol so that it doesn't settle on arterial walls. It works
well with inositol to utilize fats and cholesterol, and it also works well with methionine in
detoxifying amines, which are by-products of protein metabolism. Choline aids in the
digestion of fats, and is also very beneficial for a number of cognitive functions.


Inositol metabolizes fats and cholesterol and aids in transporting fat in the blood system.
Thus it is an aid in the redistribution of body fat. It helps to lower cholesterol levels. It is
a member of the B-Complex group and is a lipotropic agent. Inositol helps with the
breakdown of fats, and in the reduction of blood cholesterol to go along with a number of
other health benefits


L-Methionine is an amino acid and antioxidant that helps to breakdown fats, averting
build up in the liver and arteries. L-Methionine aids in lowering cholesterol. It detoxifies
amines which are by-products of protein metabolism. L-Methionine acts as a catalyst for
choline and inositol thus speeding up their function.


Betaine HCL (betaine hydrochloride) is betaine with hydrochloric acid. It is used as a
digestive aid due to its strong acidity. Betaine HCL assists the body's natural stomach
acids to ensure appropriate absorption of nutrients.


L-Carnitine has long been used by athletes for longer workout periods. It is used in fat
burners because it helps in converting stored fat into energy. It can also help in
controlling hypoglycemia and thereby benefits diabetics. A deficiency can cause
impairment of heart tissue. L-carnitine assists in the transport of fat through the cell
membrane and into the mitochondria within the cell, where these fats are oxidized to
produce the cellular energy ATP. L-Carnitine is a therapy for low energy levels,
congestive heart disease, and other diseases associated with low cellular energy.
