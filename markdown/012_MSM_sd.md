Methylsulfonylmethane (MSM) is a new dietary version of sulfur that is critically involved in
numerous reactions in practically all tissues and organs. It provides nutritional support for
healthy tendons, ligaments and muscles. Thus, it is an essential nutrient for muscle pain, such as
that caused by intense physical exercise and exertion. In addition, its role in joint swelling and
pain has only now begun to be fully appreciated.

Methylsulfonylmethane is the organic form of sulfur. In contrast, sulfur contained in sulfites, and
other sulfur derivatives used as food preservatives, is inorganic. The unique action of MSM in all
tissues is to decrease the pressure inside the cell, and to remove fluids and toxins. Presently, the
major application of MSM is for swollen and painful joints. That is quite understandable, since
sulfur is normally found in and around bone structures in the body. By the same token, MSM
relieves post-exercise muscle pain. At least some studies have shown that dietary
supplementation with MSM preserves cartilage.

Roughly one-half of the total sulfur in the body is concentrated in the muscle, skin and bones.
One of the well-known benefits of MSM as a dietary supplement is its ability to relieve pain and
inflammation. With advancing years, as the metabolic attrition in the body increases, so does the
inflammation as it tries to fight the adverse effect of normal age-related wear and tear and
increased production of free radicals. A similar situation is noted after intense exercise, which
can lead to localized inflammation. As the cells become inflamed, they swell resulting in pain.
Since MSM restores flexibility and permeability to the cell walls, fluids pass through the cell
membrane more readily, which reduces pressure, and alleviates pain. Likewise, with increased
permeability of the cell membrane, harmful substances, such as lactic acid, and toxins are
removed from the cell.

Methylsulfonylmethane has also been called the "beauty mineral." Ample supply of sulfur is
needed to keep the hair glossy and smooth, and the complexion clear and youthful. Therefore,
MSM provides a natural alternative to maintain healthy skin, nails and hair.

**FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS**

**1 What is the Daily Dosage of MSM?**
Due to its many benefits, a daily dosage of between 1000 and 3000 milligrams is usually
recommended. Of course, the optimum daily dosage depends upon the body size, age, and the
use for which MSM is expressly taken.


**2 How Should MSM Be Taken?**
It should be taken with water or any other nutritious fluid. When taking MSM, generous amounts
of healthy and nutritious fluids should be ingested.

**3 Why Does Ultimate Nutrition's Maximum MSM Contain Vitamin C?**
Vitamin C is known to stimulate the absorption of MSM. Furthermore, being free radical
scavengers, both vitamin C and MSM work in concert with each other to trigger the building of
new cells in the body. Since vitamin C is found in large amounts in fresh fruit, their intake while
on MSM has been recommended.

**4 What is the Source of MSM?**
The source of MSM is normally lignin from pine trees. Lignin is a molecule that is a part of the
cell walls in plants. The pine tree lignin is an ideal source of effective and safe MSM.

**5 What Foods Contain Natural, Organic Sulfur?**
Among the foods rich in organic sulfur are sunflower seeds, garlic, lentils, soybeans and yogurt.

**6 What Are the Contraindication?**
Individuals with kidney problems or recurrent kidney stones should not take MSM. Certain renal
tubular defects can increase the risk of recurrent kidney stones. Among other contraindications
are congenital error of kidney metabolism in which the processing of sulfur-containing amino
acids in the body is impaired.
