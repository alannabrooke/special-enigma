R-ALA


R-ALA is a natural nutrient found in the human body and some foods. In the body it is found in
mitochondria acting as a coenzyme. R-ALA serves many functions in the body; including that of
an antioxidant, booster of glutathione levels, improver of mitochondrial function, and as a
chelating agent. With all these uses, it can best be considered a vital nutrient to healthy living.
